Questions People Ask
Clear answers to help you start your work without confusion.
Do I have to pay a registration fee? +

Yes, there is a registration fee required to join, verify your ID, and get your first writing assignment. Please contact us on Telegram or WhatsApp to ask about the exact fee amount.

Who can do this work? +

Anyone! Students, housewives, or retired people. You do not need any past experience. You only need to have neat handwriting that is easy to read.

When do I get my salary? +

We pay salaries every week. After you submit your written pages, we check them within 24 to 48 hours, and then send the money straight to your bank account.

Is this a real company? +

Yes, Angelx is a genuine, MSME-approved, and MSME-registered company. We have been giving work and paying salaries since 2018.
